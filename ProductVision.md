1. Write the product vision for your team's product. The good structure for this is Greg Moore's
which is:
- Our product vision is for people aged 60 years and older.
- People aged 60 and older whose healthcare needs become more cumbersome. As we
age, there is more need for doctors' appointments, medication needs, caregiving
needs, and insurance coverage.
- We will call our product TotalCare+. It will be a website/application.
- It will be a website and application that will work in harmony with other healthcare
apps to provide all healthcare services in one easy-to-use patient portal.
- TotalCare+ will serve as a one-stop shop for patients where they can schedule
appointments, check on insurance deductibles/co-pays, search for a supplementary
plan that can provide additional coverage, track medications/request refills, etc.
- It will be different from other healthcare websites/apps that provide one or some of
these services.
- Our product will help to provide people with all the information they need to help
them monitor/maintain all possible healthcare needs.

2. What information are you using to develop the product vision (market research, academic
research, user questions, and needs)?
- To develop the product vision, we will be using academic research and market
research so that we can incorporate what the user needs or may need out of our
product. Academic research will allow us to further investigate what our product will
need to incorporate and how we can organize/create the user interface and what
information will need to be available to the users. We can also use market research to
see how we can set aside ourselves from competitors and incorporate
features/qualities that they may not be offering to their users.
